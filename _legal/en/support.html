Support

Email support@quizjet.app. Include your Telegram username or user ID so we can find your account. We answer as soon as we can.

Payments and refunds

Tell us the date of the purchase and which Premium package you bought. If you can, add a screenshot of the payment receipt Telegram showed you in the chat with the bot. Refund rules are in the Terms of Service, section 6.

Report a quiz

Send the quiz link or code and tell us what is wrong. We review every report and can remove a quiz from Discover or delete it.

Your data

To get a copy of your data or have it deleted, see section 6 of the Privacy Policy.

Using the bot

Send /help to @QuizJetBot to see the commands. Create a quiz with /newquiz or generate one with AI with /aiquiz.

Frequently asked questions

Is QuizJet free?

Yes. Creating, playing and sharing quizzes is free. Premium raises the usage limits and is bought inside Telegram with Telegram Stars; the current packages and limits are shown in the bot.

Which languages does it support?

The bot and this website come in 18 languages. The bot opens in your Telegram language; change it any time with /lang.

How does a quiz work in a group?

Add @QuizJetBot to the group. A group administrator can then start any quiz with its code or from the Discover page; if a quiz does not start, make the bot an administrator and try again. Questions are sent as Telegram quiz polls, and the members of the group see the results and the leaderboard.

Where does my data go?

Messages travel through Telegram, AI requests go to OpenAI as text only, and our servers run on AWS in Frankfurt. The Privacy Policy lists every data category, who receives it and how long it is kept.

How do I report a quiz?

Every quiz page on quizjet.app has a report link, and you can always email support@quizjet.app with the quiz link or code and what is wrong.
